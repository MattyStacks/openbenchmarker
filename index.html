Neon Flythrough

A fixed-path cinematic flight through a rain-soaked, neon-lit 1980s-movie city: instanced towers, signs, flying traffic with light trails, drones, holograms, searchlights, bloom and wet-street reflections. Runs uncapped (not limited to your monitor's refresh rate) and times every frame to GPU completion.

Plus a 3 s warm-up that is never counted. Settings are locked during the run. Keep this tab visible and don't resize the window or rotate your device, or the run is marked invalid.

Honest results

  • Measured by the run: Avg FPS, 1% Low, 5% Low, worst frame, frame count, resolution, preset, date, graphics API.
  • Provided by you: GPU (from the official catalog), OS and any optional system details. Nothing is guessed or auto-filled.
  • Results stay in this browser unless you choose to export or submit them to opengpubenchmarks.com.
