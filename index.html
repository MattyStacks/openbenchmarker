ネオン・フライスルー Benchmark 01

Neon Flythrough

A fixed, cinematic flight through a rain-soaked cyberpunk megacity. You dive between stacked towers, skim the street lanterns, climb over the rooftops and thread sky-lanes full of flying traffic, with swarms of drones, walls of neon signs and haze out to the horizon. It runs uncapped (not limited to your monitor's refresh rate), keeps the GPU fed back-to-back, and times frames with GPU timer queries where the browser supports them.

採点Score runs

Fixed tests with locked settings, so every score is the same workload. A faster or overclocked GPU scores higher. You get FPS, the score, and a breakdown of whether your GPU or CPU was the limit.

Plus a 3 s warm-up that is never counted. Settings are locked during the run. Keep this tab visible and don't resize the window or rotate your device, or the run is marked invalid.

誠実Honest results

  • Measured by the run: Avg FPS, 1% Low, 5% Low, worst frame, frame count, score (score runs), resolution, preset, date, graphics API, and what limited the run (GPU busy time, CPU time per frame, stalls).
  • Detected by the browser: WebGL renderer, CPU thread count, battery state and browser version, recorded in a separate labelled section. They never fill in your system details.
  • Provided by you: GPU (from the official catalog), OS and any optional system details. To make things easier, we sometimes suggest a value, our best guess, for details most people don't know offhand. Suggested values are always marked, and you can change or clear them.
  • Results stay in this browser unless you choose to share a link, export them or submit them to opengpubenchmarks.com.
